# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

The following activities are strictly prohibited on the ROMA Execution Bridge platform:

1. Cryptocurrency mining
2. Attacks of any kind (DDoS, brute-force, port scanning, etc.)
3. Distribution or execution of malware
4. Processing or storage of illegal content
5. Any activity that violates the laws of the Provider’s or Platform’s jurisdiction
6. Attempts to bypass security controls or gain unauthorized access

Violation of this AUP is grounds for immediate suspension of the worker and account, including forfeiture of any pending balance.
