# ROMA Execution Bridge — Provider Agreement

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-26

This Agreement governs the relationship between the Operator of the ROMA Execution Bridge platform (“Platform”) and the owner of computing resources (“Provider”).

## 1. Subject
1.1. The Provider makes idle GPUs available for ML and inference workloads.
1.2. The Platform handles job intake, routing, billing, and payouts.

## 2. Economics
2.1. Revenue from completed jobs is split as follows:
- **85%** to the Provider
- **15%** to the Platform
2.2. Payouts are made in USDT, BTC, or Kaspi (for Kazakhstan residents).
2.3. Minimum withdrawal amounts and processing times are defined in the Payout Policy.

## 3. Control and Security
3.1. The Provider retains full control over their hardware.
3.2. The Provider has the right to use the **kill-switch** at any time to immediately stop any running job.
3.3. The Platform operates in non-root mode and does not require elevated privileges.

## 4. Provider Obligations
- Maintain hardware and network availability
- Comply with the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
- Independently declare and pay all applicable taxes

## 5. Platform Obligations
- Provide the technical ability to offer GPUs
- Calculate and process payouts according to the 85/15 split
- Avoid unnecessary retention of job data

## 6. No KYC at Launch
6.1. At the initial stage the Platform does not require KYC.
6.2. The Platform reserves the right to introduce verification requirements in the future.

## 7. Limitation of Liability
7.1. The Platform does not guarantee demand or income.
7.2. The Platform is not liable for hardware downtime, power issues, third-party actions, or lost profits.
7.3. The Platform’s maximum liability is limited to the commission received in the preceding 30 days.

## 8. Term and Termination
8.1. This Agreement becomes effective upon acceptance (worker start or invite acceptance).
8.2. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time without cause.

## 9. Governing Law
Disputes shall first be resolved through good-faith negotiations. If unresolved, they shall be governed by the laws of [Jurisdiction to be specified].
