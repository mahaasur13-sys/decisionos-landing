# Payout Policy

1. **Provider share:** 85% of the value of completed jobs.
2. **Platform fee:** 15%.
3. **Supported payout methods:** USDT (TRC20/ERC20), BTC, Kaspi (Kazakhstan residents only).
4. **Minimum withdrawal:** 10 USDT (or equivalent).
5. **Processing time:** up to 3 business days after a valid withdrawal request.
6. Network fees are paid by the Provider.
7. The Platform is not a tax agent. Providers are solely responsible for declaring and paying any applicable taxes.
